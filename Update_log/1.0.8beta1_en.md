# Tuanzi's Mod 1.0.8beta1 Changelog

## Version Update

- Mod version updated to **1.0.8beta1**.

## New Features

### 1. New Void Dimension: Scavenger Corridor (`tuanzis_mod:scavenger_corridor`)
- **Environment & World Generation**:
  - Void flat world featuring dark foggy atmosphere and a default 5×5 obsidian & deepslate blast-proof safe platform with an Ender Chest.
  - Beds (sleeping/setting spawn point) and Respawn Anchors are completely disabled in this dimension.
- **Enforced Adventure Mode**:
  - Players entering the corridor are forced into Adventure mode; respawning within the corridor also strictly retains Adventure mode.

### 2. Dual Isolated Inventory & Universal Cross-Dimension Detection System
- **Universal Cross-Dimension Movement Interception**:
  - Not limited to `/corridor enter` and `/corridor leave` commands. Any form of cross-dimensional movement entering or leaving the corridor—including vanilla teleport commands (`/tp`, `/teleport`, `/execute in`), portals (Nether/End), or third-party mod items/waypoints—is automatically intercepted at the core engine level to enforce inventory swapping, state snapshotting, persistent storage, and adventure mode switching.
- **State Snapshot & Fresh Entry**:
  - Upon entry, the system captures a full snapshot of the player's prior dimension inventory (all 43 slots including hotbar, main storage, armor, and offhand), experience, health, food, saturation, exhaustion, active effects, and game mode.
  - Players are granted a clean slate with an empty inventory on their first entry.
- **Corridor Loot Persistent Storage**:
  - All items and stat progress acquired in the corridor are independently serialized in player NBT; re-entering the corridor seamlessly restores previous corridor loot.
- **Precise Coordinate Teleport Return**:
  - Running the leave command returns players back to the exact dimension, 3D coordinates (X/Y/Z), and viewing angles (Yaw/Pitch) where they left, fully restoring previous inventory and states.

### 3. Dimension-Isolated 9-Slot Ender Chest
- Ender Chests opened inside Scavenger Corridor do not share space with Overworld/Nether/End chests.
- Provides a dedicated 9-slot safe cache for valuable scavenged loot that survives player deaths in the corridor.

### 4. Enforced Death Drops & Respawn Reset
- **Forced Death Drop**: Players always drop their carried items and equipment on death in the corridor, even if `keepInventory` is enabled globally.
- **Respawn Reset**: Dying in the corridor resets the respawned player's carried items and XP, respawning at the safe platform (0.5, 65.0, 0.5) to prevent respawn-retention exploits.

### 5. New Teleport Commands
- `/corridor enter`: For players to enter the Scavenger Corridor.
- `/corridor enter <targets>`: Admin command to teleport specified players into the corridor.
- `/corridor leave`: For players to leave the corridor and return to their origin position.
- `/corridor leave <targets>`: Admin command to evacuate specified players out of the corridor.

### 6. Scavenger Corridor Exclusive "Team System"
- **Corridor Exclusive**: The team system is only active within the Scavenger Corridor. Leaving the corridor or disconnecting automatically leaves the team; leadership transfers automatically; teams with fewer than 2 players automatically disband.
- **3-Player Max Limit**: Each team holds up to 3 players.
- **Complete Friendly Fire & Debuff Immunity**: Teammates are completely immune to melee attacks, projectile hits, and direct damage caused by one another. Additionally, all harmful debuff effects (e.g., Poison, Slowness, Weakness, Nausea, Wither, Blindness) caused by teammate splash potions, lingering clouds, or tipped arrows are intercepted and blocked at the effect root, while beneficial buffs (e.g., Healing, Speed, Strength) are preserved for team sharing.
- **Complete Teammate Collision Box Removal & Projectile Phasing**:
  - Entity pushing, crowding forces, and hard collision boxes are completely eliminated between teammates on both server and client sides. Players smoothly phase through each other without blocking doorways, pushing teammates into the void, or causing jitter.
  - Conventional weapon projectiles fired by teammates (e.g., arrows, tipped arrows, tridents, snowballs) phase directly through teammates' hitboxes (`canHitEntity` returns `false`), enabling rear teammates to shoot enemies ahead without obstruction.
- **Configurable Tactical Physical Interactions (Wind Charges & Fishing Rods)**:
  - Team leaders can toggle whether teammates are affected by friendly **Wind Charges** (blast impulse/knockback) and **Fishing Rods** (hooking and pulling). Enabled by default for tactical plays and mobility setups; leaders can disable it at any time to prevent accidental displacement.
  - The check strictly distinguishes teammates from self, meaning self-mobility like wind charge rocket jumps remains fully functional regardless of the setting.
- **Long-Distance Private Glowing Outline**:
  - Automatically highlights teammates with a glowing outline when distance exceeds 20 blocks.
  - **Private Visibility**: Determined purely on the client side; the server does not broadcast any glowing flags to third parties. Non-teammates will never see this glow, guaranteeing stealth.
- **Intuitive Team Commands**:
  - `/corridor team create`: Create a team (creator becomes the leader, default glow color is green).
  - `/corridor team invite <player>`: Send a team invite to a player in the corridor (features 60s interactive clickable chat buttons `[Accept]` and `[Deny]`).
  - `/corridor team accept`: Accept a pending team invite.
  - `/corridor team deny`: Reject a pending team invite.
  - `/corridor team leave`: Leave current team.
  - `/corridor team kick <player>`: Leader can kick a member from the team.
  - `/corridor team disband`: Leader can disband the entire team.
  - `/corridor team color <color>`: Leader can customize team outline color (supports tab-completion for all 16 vanilla TeamColors).
  - `/corridor team config`: View the team configuration panel with clickable toggles for leaders.
  - `/corridor team config <wind_charge|fishing_rod|friendly_physics> <true|false>`: Leader command to explicitly toggle physical tactical interaction settings.
  - `/corridor team info` (or simply `/corridor team`): View current team leader, member list, glow color, and tactical configuration state.

### 7. Player Personal Spawn Generation & Collision Prevention
- **Dual-Axis 10,000+ Block Offsets & 500-Block Spacing**: The Central Hall `(0, 64, 0)` surrounds a protected public zone. All player personal spawn points strictly require **both X and Z coordinates to be >= 10,000 blocks** (originating from `(10000, 64, 10000)`), with spacing expanded to **500 blocks**, arranged via square corner expansion sequence.
- **Smooth Auto-Migration**: Any legacy player spawn coordinates not satisfying both X >= 10,000 and Z >= 10,000 are automatically migrated to a valid platform beyond 10,000 blocks upon entry.
- **Offline & Online Compatibility**: Indexed by lowercase username and UUID, guaranteeing seamless state retention across online/offline server switches or username changes.
- **Independent Base Platform**: Generates a 5×5 blast-resistant polished deepslate/obsidian platform with an ender chest and soul torches for each player.
- **Targeted Respawn**: Players dying inside the corridor respawn strictly on their personal spawn platforms.

### 8. Dedicated Accessory Slots & Tri-Beacon Return System
- **Corridor Exclusive Accessory Slots (Similar to Curios/Trinkets)**:
  - Separate from the vanilla 36-slot inventory with 3 dedicated slots, never drops upon death in the corridor.
  - Safely sealed alongside corridor data when leaving; invisible and unavailable outside the corridor. Automatically re-equipped upon re-entry.
  - **Inventory GUI Integration**: Opening the `E` inventory inside the corridor reveals 3 dedicated accessory slots on the left (Hall Cyan, Room Amber, Market Emerald), supporting mouseover tooltips and direct left-click activation.
- **Hall Return Beacon (`tuanzis_mod:hall_return_beacon`)**:
  - Hexagonal tower beacon with a glowing cyan crystal and orbiting brass cubes.
  - Right-click in hand or press key `[H]` (or click slot 1 in inventory): Instantly teleports to the Central Hall `(0, 64, 0)` with cyan particle and sound effects.
  - 15-second cooldown, consumes no items.
- **Room Return Beacon (`tuanzis_mod:room_return_beacon`)**:
  - Wooden plaque beacon with an amber doorway crystal and orbiting brass key.
  - Right-click in hand or press key `[R]` (or click slot 2 in inventory): Instantly teleports back to your personal spawn platform with amber gateway particle and sound effects.
  - 15-second cooldown, consumes no items.
- **Market Return Beacon (`tuanzis_mod:market_return_beacon`)**:
  - Octagonal box beacon crafted of deepslate and spruce, engraved with scales and shelves, featuring an emerald scale crystal and an orbiting brass coin.
  - Right-click in hand or press key `[M]` (or click slot 3 in inventory): Instantly teleports to the Corridor Market `(5000, 64, -5000)` with pre-generated safety platform, golden-green particles, and sound effects.
  - 15-second cooldown, consumes no items.

### 9. Dedicated Creative Tab & JEI Integration
- Added dedicated creative tab `scavenger_corridor_tab` for all Scavenger Corridor items (Hall, Room, and Market Return Beacons).
- Full JEI description pages registered for Hall Return Beacon, Room Return Beacon, and Market Return Beacon.

### 10. Axe Stripping Crafting Recipes
- **Shapeless Stripping Crafting**:
  - Added shapeless crafting recipes: Any axe (vanilla & compatible mod axes under `#minecraft:axes`) + wood block can now be crafted directly into its stripped version via player 2×2 or 3×3 crafting tables.
  - Fully supports all 25 wood variants: All logs and all-sided woods (Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, Poplar), Crimson Stem/Hyphae, Warped Stem/Hyphae, and Bamboo Blocks.
- **Durability Consumption & Remainder Mechanism**:
  - Each crafting operation consumes exactly 1 durability point on the axe, remaining safely in the crafting slot.
  - If the axe has only 1 durability left, it breaks and vanishes naturally; unbreakable axes suffer no durability damage.
  - All existing enchantments, custom names, and item components on the axe are fully preserved.
- **JEI & Recipe Book Integration**:
  - Fully integrated with JEI recipe displays, allowing players to easily view all stripping recipes.


### 11. Scavenger Corridor Supply Block: Black Box Data Core Case (`blackbox_core_case`)
- **Appearance & Scale**:
  - Rectangular compact electronic supply case (~1.0 L × 0.7 W × 0.8 H blocks), combining a reinforced server chassis and flight black box, strictly adhering to vanilla low-poly voxel standards.
  - Matte black alloy body, dark silver metallic corner protectors with embedded cyan light strips, front terminal monitor, 6 status LEDs, and a circular decode interface.
  - Short reinforced top antenna with a pulsating floating blue energy dot; side worn barcode label with a red confidential stamp.
- **Ambient Feedback, Multi-layered Audio & Physics Particles**:
  - Subtle hard drive rotation and electrical hum when nearby players approach.
  - **Acoustic Unlocking Trilogy**: Synchronously triggers pneumatic depressurization hiss, nested mechanical panel sliding and latching sounds, and an ascending low-frequency core activation drone.
  - **1.5-Second Particle Geyser**: Driven by block entity tick, a 1.5-second continuous upward geyser of cyan data streams and electric sparks erupts from the opening, while dense white cold air particles diffuse horizontally along the bottom rim.
- **Three-Layer Nested Mechanical Animation (GeckoLib Powered)**:
  - **Three-Layer Sequential Sliding Mechanism**:
    1. Outermost matte black alloy faceplate slides downwards by 0.2 blocks;
    2. Middle horizontal ventilation grilles slide apart to the left and right flanks;
    3. Innermost reinforced glass inspection window hinges upwards, fully revealing the hollow chamber.
  - **Core Illumination & Screen Interaction**: Cold white core light initially leaks through the hairline seam, then blooms into full radiant illumination; the terminal screen flashes "CORE ACCESS" before powering down.
  - **Antenna Energy Burst**: The floating blue antenna beacon dot fractures into 8 micro-sparks scattering outward before fading.
  - **Persistent Opened Pose**: Powered by `GeoBlockEntity` networking; once unlocked, `thenPlayAndHold` locks the block model in the unfolded opened state permanently across world reloads.
- **Functionality**:
  - Provides 27 secure storage slots for intelligence and electronic supplies. Registered under the dedicated `scavenger_corridor_tab` creative tab with full JEI page integration.
- **Bug Fixes**:
  - Fixed GeckoLib 5 resource registration by directing to standard `geckolib/models` and `geckolib/animations` paths, eliminating model/animation not found errors and missing textures.
  - Fixed raw translation keys showing up for BlockItems in inventories and the creative tab, completing full bilingual DataGen coverage.

### 12. Soulbound Enchantment Wither Slaying Route & Kill Tracking System
- **Damage Tracking & Wither Glowing Feedback**:
  - Introduced global damage tracking for Withers, monitoring total damage taken alongside aggregate player damage output.
  - When all players combined account for **>= 90%** of a Wither's total damage taken, the Wither gains the glowing effect to visually indicate that player damage criteria are satisfied. If outside non-player damage causes player share to drop below 90%, glowing is immediately removed.
- **Valid Kill Progression & Milestone Reward**:
  - Requires only that **overall player damage share is >= 90%**. The kill point is awarded directly to whoever lands the killing blow on the Wither (the killer does not individually need >= 90% damage).
  - Displays round progress (e.g. `Wither Kills Progress: 1/1000`) in the action bar after each successful kill.
  - Upon reaching each **1,000 kills** milestone (1000, 2000, 3000...), 1 *Soulbound* Enchanted Book (Level 1) drops beside the killer, accompanied by a challenge complete sound and a gold chat announcement.
  - Kill statistics are persisted in player data and preserved across deaths and dimensions via `restoreFrom`.
- **New Query & Admin Commands (`/witherkills`)**:
  - `/witherkills`: Checks personal total valid Wither kills and remaining kills until the next Soulbound drop.
  - `/witherkills query <targets>`: Admin command (OP permission) to view another player's kill stats and next drop progress.
  - `/witherkills add <targets> <amount>`: Admin command to increment a player's kill counter.
  - `/witherkills set <targets> <amount>`: Admin command to set a player's kill counter.
  - `/witherkills reset <targets>`: Admin command to reset a player's kill counter to 0.
- **JEI Page Integration**:
  - Registered a dedicated JEI information page for Soulbound enchanted books, clearly explaining the dual acquisition pathways: Warden drop (>50% damage share) and Wither slaying milestone (player group total damage >=90%, final blow awarded to killer, 1 book per 1,000 kills), along with command instructions.
