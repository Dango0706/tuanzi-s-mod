# Tuanzi's Mod 1.0.8-beta3 Changelog

---

## Version Update

- Mod version officially updated to **1.0.8-beta3**.

## Item & Gameplay Optimizations

- **Overworld Build Height Limit Expansion & Hot-Reload Configuration**:
  - Expanded the Overworld (`minecraft:overworld`) underlying safe height capacity to the vanilla engine limit (`Y = 2031`, upper bound `2032`) without altering vanilla Overworld terrain generation, the Nether, or the End.
  - Added `overworldMaxBuildHeight` to `config/tuanzis_mod/config.json` (valid range **320 ~ 2032**, default **2032**; `320` corresponds to vanilla max placeable `Y = 319`, and `2032` corresponds to `Y = 2031`).
  - Supports restart-free hot-reloading with automatic client synchronization via:
    1. **Commands**: `/tuanzis_mod config get overworldMaxBuildHeight`, `/tuanzis_mod config set overworldMaxBuildHeight <320..2032>`, and `/tuanzis_mod config reload`.
    2. **ModMenu Config Screen**: Added a numeric input field and quick `Min 320` / `Max 2032` preset buttons that apply immediately in singleplayer.
    3. **Background File Auto-Reload**: Added a `WatchService`-based file watcher (`ModConfigWatcher`) that automatically detects external edits to `config/tuanzis_mod/config.json` and hot-reloads/syncs the new limit.
- **Potion of Undying Stack Size Increase**: Increased the maximum stack size of all Potion of Undying types (Normal Drinkable, Splash, and Lingering, including both standard and extended variants) from 1 to **16**.
- **Luck Synergy with Casino Win Rates**:
  - Player Luck (`minecraft:luck`) now affects win rates across Casino games (Roulette, Duck Derby solo/legacy races, Slot Machine, Fortune Wheel, Plinko, Blackjack, and Poker).
  - Each **1 point of Luck** increases win rate by **+0.5%**, while each **1 point of negative Luck** decreases win rate by **-0.5%**.
- **Leader Zombie XP Drop Optimization**:
  - Killing a Leader Zombie (possessing `minecraft:leader_zombie_bonus`, including Zombies, Husks, Drowned, Zombie Villagers, etc.) now awards base XP equal to **Max Health / (2.5 ~ 4.5)**.
  - If the Leader Zombie is a **baby**, the XP reward is **doubled**.

## New Enchantments: Auspicious & Ill Omen

- **Auspicious (`auspicious`)**:
  - Helmet non-treasure enchantment with **UNCOMMON** rarity and max level **IV**.
  - Grants **+0.5 Luck** per level while worn on the head (**+2.0 Luck** at Level IV).
  - Mutually exclusive with Ill Omen; obtainable via Enchanting Tables, loot chests, Librarian villager trades, and Creative mode inventory, with a dedicated JEI info page.
- **Ill Omen (`ill_omen`)**:
  - Helmet **Curse** enchantment with **UNCOMMON** rarity and max level **IV** (red curse display name; cannot be removed via Grindstone).
  - Can still be rolled directly on the **Enchanting Table** (also available from loot chests, Librarian villager trades, and Creative mode inventory).
  - Reduces Luck by **-0.5** per level while worn on the head (**-2.0 Luck** at Level IV), is mutually exclusive with Auspicious, and has a dedicated JEI info page.

## Weapon Adjustments & Enhancements

- **Fractured Void Scar (`rift_scar`)**: Now innately grants **+0.25 Sweeping Damage Ratio** (`Attributes.SWEEPING_DAMAGE_RATIO` mainhand attribute modifier), improving AoE sweep damage and stacking additively with the Sweeping Edge enchantment (up to the vanilla cap of 1.0).

## New Features: Potion of Purifying & Potion of Desperate Stand

- **Potion of Purifying / Purifying Pulse (`purifying` / `long_purifying` -> `purifying_pulse`)**:
  - Includes Normal (45s), Extended (90s), Splash, Lingering, and Tipped Arrow variants (no crafting/brewing recipe yet; added to Creative tab & JEI).
  - Instantly clears all milk-clearable harmful status effects on application while preserving beneficial and neutral effects.
  - Automatically removes 1 active milk-clearable harmful effect every 5 seconds, and reduces the duration of newly acquired milk-clearable harmful effects by **-50%**.
  - Does not affect Adrenaline Overdraw or non-effect mechanics such as Boss Overheat gauges.
- **Potion of Desperate Stand / Desperate Stand (`desperate_stand`)**:
  - Lasts 30 seconds with no extended variant (includes Splash, Lingering, and Tipped Arrow variants; no crafting/brewing recipe yet; added to Creative tab & JEI).
  - Grants **+10% melee damage** while active; incoming damage is deferred as **Trauma** instead of reducing health immediately.
  - Each hostile mob killed while active reduces Trauma by **10% of the mob's max health**.
  - 1 tick after the effect ends (even if cleared early), remaining Trauma > 0 inflicts equal **true damage** (bypasses armor, resistance, and absorption; lethal, though Totems of Undying and Potions of Undying can still save you).
  - Added a persistent **white-heart Trauma Bar HUD** 2px above the health bar (`Trauma 37 | 18s`, omitting `.0` for whole numbers, showing empty hearts + countdown when Trauma is 0), with a ModConfig option to place it `2px Above Conflicting HUD` or `Right Middle of Screen` when overlapping other hotbar HUDs.

## Blueprint System Improvements & New Features

- **3D Blueprint Inspector Block Replacement (`BlueprintBlockReplaceScreen`)**:
  - Updated the right-hand sidebar title to **"Block List (Double-click to Replace)"** and widened the sidebar accordingly.
  - Double-clicking any block entry in the right-hand material list opens a dedicated block picker dialog featuring a top search bar and Creative-mode style category tabs underneath (automatically hiding empty non-block tabs and providing special entries for Air, Water Source, and Lava Source in the "All Blocks" tab).
  - Confirming a replacement preserves compatible block state properties (such as `facing`, `half`, `slab type`, `waterlogged`), removes mismatched `BlockEntities` NBT entries, and immediately refreshes the 3D preview, material counts, active world ghost, and Blueprint Table data/slot item; clicking Cancel leaves everything untouched.
  - Added universal multi-block compatibility checks that prevent replacing multi-block structures (doors, beds, mod multiblocks) with single blocks or incompatible multiblock types and notify the player clearly; replacing a fluid targets its source blocks while clearing associated flowing cells to air.
- **Universal Fluid Source-Only Material Counting**:
  - Updated `BlueprintBuildPlanner` to check `FluidState#isSource()` universally across all vanilla and modded fluids so that material stats, total block counts, and Blueprint Cannon consumption only count fluid source blocks while excluding flowing liquid cells (preserving full flowing liquid rendering in the 3D preview and normal counting for waterlogged blocks).
- **Blueprint Cannon Bounds & Parser Safety Fixes**:
  - Added max range (`256` blocks), world valid bounds (`Level#isInValidBounds`), and loaded-chunk checks (`Level#isLoaded` / `hasChunkAt`) across `BlueprintCannonBlockEntity`, `BlueprintBuildPlanner`, and `LaunchedItem`, preventing distant targets from synchronously force-loading/generating chunks and preventing out-of-height targets from consuming materials/fuel without placing blocks.
  - Fixed 32-bit `int` overflow in `width * length` (`ArithmeticException: / by zero` when `Width = Length = 65536`) across `BlueprintBuildPlanner`, `BlueprintDirectPlacer`, and blueprint preview helpers, and added `y >= height` truncation when `Blocks` exceeds `Width * Height * Length`.
- **Creative Core Firing Speed GUI & Blueprint Cannon Rate Customization**:
  - Right-clicking while holding a Creative Core now opens a dedicated configuration screen (`CreativeCoreScreen`) to customize the Blueprint Cannon's firing speed (`1~64` shots every `1~1200` ticks; defaults to `1` shot every `5` ticks), with live speed display in the item tooltip and updated JEI guide.
  - `BlueprintCannonBlockEntity` now reads the firing interval and batch shot count from any Creative Core detected in nearby containers and fires blocks/entities accordingly.

## New Features: Decorative Coin Exchange Recipes

Added **23 shaped crafting & reverse-splitting recipes** for the decorative coin series (13 forward consolidation recipes + 10 reverse splitting recipes). All coin recipes use shaped layouts to distinguish upgrade vs. downgrade directions and prevent conflicts with 1:1 chip conversion or same-count forward recipes:

### 1. Layout Rules
- **Forward Consolidation (Horizontal / Row-first)**:
  - 2-coin upgrades: placed horizontally (`XX`), craftable in the 2×2 inventory grid.
  - 5-coin upgrades: placed row-first in 3×2 (`XXX` / `XX `).
  - Mixed-denomination upgrades: `ABB` / `BBB` (1× 5 Yuan + 5× 1 Yuan) or `AAB` (2× 20 Yuan + 1× 10 Yuan, 2× 200 Yuan + 1× 100 Yuan).
- **Reverse Splitting (Single-slot / Vertical)**:
  - **Coins without matching chips (5, 10, 20, 200 Yuan)**: 1 coin (`X`) splits directly into the lower denomination.
  - **Coins with matching chips (100, 1000, 5000, 10000, 50000, 100000 Yuan)**: Because 1× coin is reserved for 1:1 shapeless chip conversion, reverse splitting uses **2 coins stacked vertically** (`X` / `X`) to output 2× the base ratio (`4×` or `10×` lower-denomination coins), avoiding any collision with horizontal `XX` forward recipes.
- **Mixed-Denomination Recipes**: `1× 5 Yuan + 5× 1 Yuan -> 10 Yuan`, `2× 20 Yuan + 1× 10 Yuan -> 50 Yuan`, and `2× 200 Yuan + 1× 100 Yuan -> 500 Yuan` are forward-only (no reverse recipes generated).

## Bug Fixes & Stability Improvements

### 1. Dedicated Server Compatibility & Side Isolation
- **Client Mixin Separation**: Moved client-only `AnvilScreenMixin` and `GuiMixin` out of the common `tuanzis_mod.mixins.json` into `tuanzis_mod.client.mixins.json` to prevent `ClassNotFoundException` crashes on Dedicated Server startup.
- **Common/Server Client-Reference Decoupling**: Split client-side double-jump networking and effects into `ClientDoubleJumpHandler`, and introduced `ScavengerClientBridge` for `ScavengerEquipmentSlot` and `ScavengerEquipmentContainer`, eliminating `NoClassDefFoundError` crashes on Dedicated Servers.

### 2. Item Duplication, Loss Prevention & Data Persistence
- **Anvil Stack Duplication & Rainbow Sponge Fixes**: Fixed an infinite item duplication exploit when renaming full stacks or crafting Rainbow Sponges on an Anvil by tracking whether left-slot remainder restoration applies; hardened Rainbow Sponge `&q<n>` gradient count parsing (`[1, 128]`), filtered empty color segments to prevent `/ by zero`, and required `cost > 0` in `mayPickup`.
- **Block Breaker Piston Move Duplication Fix**: Prevented item duplication when moving `BlockBreakerBlockEntity` via regular/sticky pistons, chained pushes, or 1-tick short pulses by skipping drops in `preRemoveSideEffects` during piston moves, attaching `UPDATE_SKIP_BLOCK_ENTITY_SIDEEFFECTS` in `PistonMovingBlockEntityMixin`, and atomically consuming migration data in `BlockBreakerPistonManager`.
- **Machine Comparator Refresh**: Removed dead `getBlockEntity` checks in `affectNeighborsAfterRemoval` across `BlockBreakerBlock`, `BlockPlacerBlock`, and `PlayerSimulatorBlock`, calling `Containers.updateNeighboursAfterDestroy` directly so comparators reset to 0 immediately upon removal.
- **Blackbox Core Case Drops & Revealed State Persistence**: Fixed multi-cell items dropping duplicated copies on break by returning stacks only on the top-left anchor slot and dropping each entry once in `preRemoveSideEffects`; completed `RevealedPlayers` deserialization in `ScavengerLootContainerState`.
- **Blueprint Cannon Fuel Pre-check**: Checked `hasFuel` before consuming nearby container materials in `BlueprintCannonBlockEntity#processEntityStep` so entity materials are not lost when pausing on empty fuel.
- **Traveler's Notebook & Craftsman's Charm Offhand & Slot Locking**: Fixed offhand usage binding to the mainhand and corrupting mainhand NBT, locked the active held slot against dropping/moving/swapping while open, and added server-side guards in `removed()`.
- **Gacha Ticket Consumption Order**: Moved `stack.shrink(1)` after the `results.isEmpty()` check in `GachaItem` so empty/invalid pools do not consume tickets.
- **World Sculptor's Pen Legal Stack Splitting**: Split returned/dropped items by `getDefaultMaxStackSize()` during large undo operations to avoid creating illegal `ItemStack`s with count > 99.
- **Soul Merchant Station Immediate Break**: Updated `releaseVillager` to check whether the villager is already in the `ServerLevel` rather than `isAlive()`, preventing villager loss when breaking the station on the same tick.

### 3. Scavenger Corridor System Fixes
- **Loadout, Swap & Stash Security**: Prevented unequipped backpacks from automatically re-equipping; verified free space before clearing old slots when swapping safeboxes/backpacks; preserved remainder counts when equipping stacked items from hotbar/offhand; enforced active search session & revealed checks when equipping from `CONTAINER` and preserved `entryId` on armor rollback; validated an open `BaseStashMenu` for `BASE_STASH` packet actions.
- **Base Upgrade Station Independent Cache**: Maintained a dedicated `baseStashEntries` client cache and synced it automatically when opening `BaseUpgradeStationMenu` so material counts display accurately without opening the Base Stash Cabinet first.
- **Tactical Container Auto-Close**: Added `!container.isContainerRemoved()` to `ScavengerTacticalMenu#stillValid`.

### 4. Combat, Status Effects, Enchantments & Calculations
- **Adrenaline Expiration CME Fix**: Deferred applying Weakness/Mining Fatigue and removing Overdraw until `LivingEntity#tick` (outside `tickEffects` iteration) to prevent `ConcurrentModificationException` from swallowing other active effects.
- **Single-Pass Sonic Boom & Earth Pile Hammer Damage**: Replaced `SonicBoomBlockMixin` `hurtServer` re-entry with a single `@ModifyVariable` `0.85F` multiplier and proper Mixin priority ordering; removed the redundant manual `DamageCalculator` call in `EarthPileHammerItem#triggerEarthShockwave` so damage and hit hooks only evaluate once.
- **Chain Pain Friendly-Fire Immunity**: Passed `@Nullable Entity attacker` through `ChainPainDamageSource` so Scavenger Corridor teammates remain immune to splash damage.
- **Star Rift Blade Global Tick Buff**: Moved attack-speed buff countdown from `inventoryTick` to `ServerTickEvents.END_SERVER_TICK` per entity so multiple blades do not expire early and modifiers do not persist after dropping the blade.
- **Decoy Totem Armor Alignment**: Removed duplicate base armor assignment in `DecoyTotemItem` that doubled armor when copying player equipment.
- **Void Anchor Cross-Dimension Fallback**: Tracked safe positions per dimension and resolved `targetLevel` from `globalPos.dimension()` on respawn fallback.
- **Flight Effect Spectator Compatibility**: Excluded `isSpectator()` players in `FlightEffectMixin` so spectator flight is not revoked when the Flight effect expires.
- **Wolf Talisman Persistence & `/kill` Bypass**: Persisted downed and rotten-flesh feeding state to wolf NBT, and allowed `DamageTypeTags.BYPASSES_INVULNERABILITY` (`/kill` and void damage) through the downed invulnerability check.

### 5. Networking, GUI, Mechanics, Commands & World State
- **32-bit `ContainerData` Splitting (6 Menu Pairs)**: Split and reassembled 32-bit `int` values into high/low 16-bit shorts across `BlueprintCannon`, `BlockBreaker`, `BlockPlacer`, `BaseUpgradeStation`, `BaseStash`, and `ScavengerTactical` menus/block entities to prevent truncation overflow at `|X/Z| > 32767` or >32767-block blueprints.
- **Large Compressed Blueprint Packets**: Switched `GhostPlaceStructurePacket` and `BlueprintTableImportPacket` to `COMPRESSED_COMPOUND_TAG` and registered them via `registerLarge` to prevent disconnects on large blueprints.
- **Blueprint Cannon Packet Reach Validation**: Added loaded-chunk and 8-block reach validation for `BlueprintCannonActionPacket` and `BlueprintMaterialBookPacket`.
- **Build Height Limit Decoupling**: Lowering `overworldMaxBuildHeight` now only blocks placing new blocks above the limit while still allowing breaking and interacting with pre-existing high-altitude blocks.
- **Manager Lifecycle Cleanup & Channeling Validation**: Cleared `TempBlockManager` and `TeleportManager` static state on `SERVER_STOPPED` to prevent cross-world leaks and cooldown pollution; added loaded-chunk checks to `TempBlockManager`; validated dimension stability and held charged Traveler's Notebook during/at the end of `TeleportManager` channeling.
- **Gacha Sub-menu Sync & Button Protection**: Recreated parent menus with fresh `containerId`s when returning from `GachaImportMenu` / `GachaWeightEditMenu`, and blocked `PICKUP_ALL` on `slotIndex >= 54` so double-clicking cannot pull control buttons.
- **Player Simulator Quasi-Connectivity**: Unified `isPowered(level, pos)` to check both `pos` and `pos.above()` during `serverTick`.
- **Targeted Piston GUI Close**: `PistonBaseBlockMixin` now only closes `BlockBreakerMenu` instances viewing the specific moved container instead of all players in the dimension.
- **Blueprint Command & `.schem` Parser Bounds**: Used `long` multiplication for `BlueprintCommand` volume checks to prevent `int` overflow crashes, and added positive dimension/volume and `]` closing checks in `LitematicaParser`.
