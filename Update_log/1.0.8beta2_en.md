## Version Update

- Mod version officially updated to **1.0.8beta2**.

## New Features

### 1. New Portable Storage Item: Golden Pocket (`tuanzis_mod:golden_pocket`)
- **Massive Portable Storage**:
  - Right-click while holding to open a portable 6x9 (54 slots, large chest capacity) inventory space anywhere.
  - Item data is stored independently inside the item's `DataComponents.CONTAINER` component, supporting portable carrying, player trading, and retention on death/drop.
- **Strict Anti-Nesting Restrictions**:
  - Cannot place other Golden Pockets inside a Golden Pocket.
  - Cannot place any Shulker Boxes inside (covering undyed, 16 colored variants, and modded shulker boxes).
- **Duplication Prevention & Safe Interaction**:
  - The held inventory/hotbar slot containing the active Golden Pocket is strictly locked during GUI interaction to prevent item movement, dropping, or numeric hotbar swapping.
  - Container screen title prioritizes custom anvil naming (defaults to "Golden Pocket").
- **Deep Seamless Litematica Integration**:
  - Full compatibility with Litematica and MaLiLib. The Material List automatically detects and tallies all building materials contained within Golden Pockets in the player's inventory.
  - Automatically excludes the Golden Pocket itself from building material counts.
  - Uses conditional Mixin configuration to ensure safe and transparent fallback when Litematica is not installed.
- **Creative Tab & JEI Support**:
  - Added to the main mod creative tab (`TUANZIS_MOD_TAB`) and vanilla Tools & Utilities tab (`TOOLS_AND_UTILITIES`).
  - Integrated with dedicated JEI description pages explaining anti-nesting and schematic integration features.

### 2. World Sculptor's Pen & Golden Pocket Smart Consumption Integration
- **Deep Multi-Level Inventory Material Consumption**:
  - When batch filling blocks (except Air Only mode), the World Sculptor's Pen automatically scans and consumes target blocks stored inside any Golden Pockets in the player's inventory, alongside normal inventory and shulker boxes.
  - Automatically updates the pocket's `DataComponents.CONTAINER` component in real time upon block consumption.
- **Smart Material Restitution on Undo**:
  - When undoing a fill operation within 60 seconds, refunded blocks are intelligently placed back into available slots within the Golden Pocket before spilling over into the player's main inventory.

    $$E(x, y) = \max\left(300 - 50y, \left\lfloor 1 + 30969 \left[ \min\left(\frac{x}{15y}, \frac{2}{15}\right)^2 + \left(1 - \left(\frac{2}{15}\right)^2\right) \frac{\max\left(0, \frac{x}{15y} - \frac{2}{15}\right)}{1 - \frac{2}{15}} \right] \right\rfloor\right)$$
