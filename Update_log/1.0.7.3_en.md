# Tuanzi's Mod 1.0.7.3 Changelog

## Bug Fixes & Mechanics Corrections

- **Enchantment Bonus & Penalty Calculation Fix: Weather & Diurnal Blades**:
  - **Additive Bonus Model Rectification**:
    - Fixed an issue where Rain Blade, Frost Blade, Thunder Blade, Sun Blade, Day Blade, and Night Blade erroneously multiplied the weapon's base attack damage by the bonus factor.
    - Strictly corrected to flat additive base attack damage bonuses:
      - **Rain Blade**: Adds `Level * 1.15 + 1.0` base damage in rainy weather (Level I +2.15, Level V +6.75).
      - **Frost Blade**: Adds `Level * 1.15 + 1.0` base damage in snowy weather (Level I +2.15, Level V +6.75).
      - **Thunder Blade**: Adds `Level * 1.45 + 1.0` base damage in thunderstorm/thundersnow weather (Level I +2.45, Level V +8.25).
      - **Sun Blade**: Adds `Level * 1.15 + 1.0` base damage in clear weather (Level I +2.15, Level V +6.75).
      - **Day Blade**: Adds `Level * 0.675 + 0.2` base damage in daytime (Level I +0.875, Level V +3.575).
      - **Night Blade**: Adds `Level * 0.675 + 0.2` base damage in nighttime (Level I +0.875, Level V +3.575).
  - **Flat Point Penalty Reduction Fix**:
    - Fixed an issue where the penalty reduction for weather enchantments during inactive weather was erroneously calculated as a percentage of weapon base damage (e.g., -81.25% at Level I).
    - Corrected to flat numerical base damage reductions (`amount = Math.max(1.0f, amount - penalty)`, with minimum 1.0 damage threshold):
      - **Rain Blade**: Decreases `0.1625 * (6 - Level)` base damage in dry weather (Level I -0.8125, Level V -0.1625).
      - **Frost Blade**: Decreases `0.1625 * (6 - Level)` base damage in non-snowy weather (Level I -0.8125, Level V -0.1625).
      - **Thunder Blade**: Decreases `0.1825 * (6 - Level)` base damage in non-thunderstorm weather (Level I -0.9125, Level V -0.1825).
      - **Sun Blade**: Decreases `0.1625 * (6 - Level)` base damage in non-clear weather (Level I -0.8125, Level V -0.1625).
  - **Documentation & Ecosystem Parity**:
    - Synchronized all JEI descriptions and DataGen language entries to display exact additive bonus and flat penalty values.
