# Tuanzi's Mod 1.0.7.1 Changelog

## New Features & Gacha Pool

- **New Weapon Type: Nodachi**:
  - **Full Tier Support**: Introduced Nodachi in all 6 tiers: Wooden, Stone, Iron, Golden, Diamond, and Netherite.
  - **Base Stats**: Unified attack speed of 1.3, extended attack reach of 3.6 blocks (backed by Minecraft 26.3 `ATTACK_RANGE` component and `ENTITY_INTERACTION_RANGE` +0.6). Base damages mirror vanilla sword scaling (Wood/Gold 3.0, Stone 4.0, Iron 5.0, Diamond 6.0, Netherite 7.0).
  - **Core Mechanics (Momentum Rhythm Combo)**:
    - **Arc Metronome UI & QTE Rhythm Window**: When holding a Nodachi, the crosshair indicator is replaced by an arc metronome needle swinging left and right at a constant speed (never accelerating through the highlight). The swing speed shifts each full cycle, and the highlighted rhythm window covers a randomized **20% ~ 30%** of the track. Once attack cooldown reaches 100%, striking as the needle sweeps through the highlight chains into the next tier seamlessly:
      - **Tier 1**: Attack speed boosts to 1.5, Attack damage +1;
      - **Tier 2**: Attack speed boosts to 1.7, Attack damage +2;
      - **Tier 3 (Max)**: Attack speed hits 1.9, regular attacks unleash a piercing 4-block **Cleave Aura** (5s internal cooldown, does not trigger exhaustion).
    - **Penalty Optimizations**: Missing swings on air or failing to hit an entity within 1.5s clears all Momentum and causes a **0.5-second** exhaustion weakness (prevents attacking). Taking damage no longer interrupts or resets Momentum.
  - **Blade "Sweet Spot" Detection**:
    - **Blade Tip (>= 2.2 blocks)**: Deals 100% full damage and accumulates Momentum.
    - **Middle Blade (1.5 ~ 2.2 blocks)**: Deals 85% damage (handled by `DamageCalculator`) and maintains current Momentum (cannot build Momentum).
    - **Close Hilt (0 ~ 1.5 blocks)**: Damage reduced by 25% (handled by `DamageCalculator`), cannot build Momentum, and knockback reduced by 75%. Encourages maintaining optimal spacing.
  - **Sweeping Variations & Projectile**:
    - **0 ~ 1 Momentum**: Sweeps a 120° sector with 2.8-block radius, knocking back minor mobs with 0 sweep damage.
    - **2 Momentum**: Sweeps an expanded 150° sector with 3.4-block radius, dealing 1 cleave damage to secondary targets.
    - **3 Momentum (Cleave Aura)**: Regular attacks release a crescent energy wave travelling at 20 blocks/s up to 4 blocks (2.5 blocks wide) with 5s internal cooldown, dealing 20% weapon damage and cleaving through cobwebs, bamboo, and vegetation.
  - **Minimal Lore & Dedicated UI**: Cluttered hover tooltip lore is removed; all combat feedback is elegantly presented by the arc metronome and momentum indicators beneath the crosshair.
  - **Full Mod Ecosystem**: Available in Creative Mode tab, diagonal crafting recipes, netherite smithing upgrade, 32×32 pixel art textures, and JEI overview pages.

  - **JEI Description Updated**: Updated the Paint Bucket JEI information page to include the Shift + Right-Click quick color picking instructions.

- **Changelog Display Safe Truncation**:
  - **First 1000 Characters Safe Display**: When parsing and displaying changelogs in the update confirmation screen, if the content exceeds 1000 characters, only the first 1000 characters are displayed, with the remaining content replaced by `...更多内容请查看modrinth/腾讯文档`.
  - **Launch Stability Assurance**: Prevents main thread freeze or game launch crash caused by excessive font splitting and rendering calculations when displaying overly long changelogs.
  - **New "View Detailed Changelog" Button**: Added a dedicated "查看详细更新日志" (View Detailed Changelog) button to the update prompt screen, navigating directly to the Tencent Docs online changelog  when clicked for complete update details.

- **Codex of Enchanting Lapis Lazuli Consumption Optimization**:
  - **Survival Lapis Cost**: Rerolling enchantments via the Codex of Enchanting in Survival mode now additionally consumes 1 Lapis Lazuli from the enchanting table's lapis slot (alongside 1 experience level); rerolling is disabled if no lapis lazuli is present in the slot. Free in Creative mode as before.
  - **GUI Button & Tooltip Sync**: The "Reroll Enchantments" button status and tooltip now dynamically reflect lapis availability; when missing lapis, the button is disabled with an informative tooltip ("Not enough lapis lazuli (Requires 1 Lapis in slot)") to prevent misclicks.
  - **Item Tooltips & JEI Description Updated**: Updated tooltips and the JEI info page to accurately describe the new lapis lazuli requirement.

- **Crossbow Power Enchantment Compatibility**:
  - **Survival Enchanting Support**: Crossbows can now receive the "Power" enchantment in Survival mode directly via Enchanting Tables (rolls Power I–V) and Anvils (merging Power enchanted books). Strictly scoped to Power only, without leaking Flame, Punch, or Infinity onto crossbows.
  - **Halved Damage Scaling Balance**: When firing arrows that hit an entity, the Power enchantment provides half the damage bonus of a regular Bow. Vanilla Bow Power bonus formula is `0.5 + 0.5 * Level` (+1.0 at Level I, +3.0 at Level V), so the Crossbow Power bonus is halved to `0.25 + 0.25 * Level` (+0.5 at Level I, +1.5 at Level V base arrow damage).
  - **Ranged Hit Scoping**: Bonus damage applies strictly when projectile arrows hit entities, never triggering during melee strikes with a crossbow.
  - **Ecosystem & Unified Calculations**: All damage bonuses are routed through `DamageCalculator.getCrossbowPowerBonus` and traced with `ModLog.debug`; registered dedicated JEI mechanics information pages for crossbows and Power enchanted books.

## Bug Fixes

- **Ancient Scroll - Blood Leech Fix**:
  - Fixed an issue where attacking a Trial Dummy with a weapon enchanted with "Ancient Scroll - Blood Leech" would erroneously trigger lifesteal, restoring player health and spawning heart particles. Dummy targets are now properly excluded.

- **Trial Dummy Projectile Hit & Damage Attribution Optimization**:
  - **No Disappearance on Projectile Damage**: Fixed an issue where attacking a Trial Dummy with ranged projectiles (bows, crossbows, tridents, etc.) in Creative mode erroneously triggered creative instant-break destruction. Overrode death logic to protect against unexpected death from extreme overkill damage.
  - **Accurate Projectile Source Attribution**: Introduced smart projectile origin resolution (`resolveSourceEntity`), fully supporting players, mobs (e.g. skeletons), and dispensers as damage sources; attacks from players are correctly protected against durability loss.
  - **Arrow Recovery & Trident Safety**: Survival mode projectile hits accurately refund the exact fired arrow (via `getPickupItemStackOrigin()`, preserving tipped/spectral arrows) without cluttering creative mode with drops; resolved the critical bug where thrown tridents were erroneously deleted and replaced with wooden arrows.
  - **Unified Damage Diagnostics & Floating Numbers**: Full defense mitigation calculation accounting for armor, toughness, resistance, and protection enchantments, logging detailed diagnostics via `ModLog.debug`; broadcasts floating damage numbers (`TrialDummyDamagePacket`) to nearby players and provides real-time ActionBar DPS feedback.

- **Nodachi QTE Rhythm & Momentum Accumulation Fix**:
  - **Fixed Unintended Momentum Accumulation Outside QTE Window**: Completely removed incorrect server-side full-charge fallback logic that mistakenly marked any fully charged attack as on-beat, ensuring Momentum only accumulates when hitting within genuine rhythm windows.
  - **Robust Network Synchronization & Grace Period Management**: Reset rhythm grace ticks immediately upon receiving off-beat signals; added client pre-attack state validation (`missTime` and `isHandsBusy`) to prevent invalid swings from triggering desynced signals.
  - **Precision Alignment Between HUD & Hit Detection**: Aligned arc metronome UI visual highlight feedback with hit detection tolerance, eliminating floating-point jitter and ensuring what you see on the crosshair matches exact timing.
