# Tuanzi's Mod 1.0.7 Changelog

## New Items
- **New Item: Chromatic Skull**:
  - A mysterious skull glowing with shifting rainbow colors, bearing the spiritual imprint connecting across dimensions.
  - **Right-Click Interaction**: Right-click while holding the Chromatic Skull to open a dedicated username input panel in the center of the screen.
  - **Player Head Acquisition & Visual Preview**:
    - Enter any valid official Minecraft player username (3–16 characters). The panel validates the username dynamically.
    - **Two-Step Confirmation**: After typing a valid username, clicking [Confirm] (or pressing Enter) loads and displays an authentic preview of the player skull (including a 48x48 high-fidelity face portrait and a 3D player head item badge, with hover tooltip), while dynamically switching the button to [Claim Skull].
    - **Acquisition**: Clicking [Claim Skull] (or pressing Enter again) verifies the request with the server, consumes 1 held Chromatic Skull, and gives you the corresponding player's official skin head accompanied by a sound effect.
    - **Dynamic Reset on Name Change**: Modifying the username in the input box immediately resets the button back to [Confirm] and returns the preview to the "Unconfirmed" state, preventing unintentional acquisitions.
  - **Safe Cancellation**: Click [Cancel] or press Esc to close the panel with no effect or item consumption.
  - **Acquisition**: Currently unobtainable through normal survival gameplay (available directly in the creative inventory tab).
  - **JEI Support**: Registered with full JEI info description pages.
- **New Item: Codex of Enchanting**:
  - A deep blue mystical tome imbued with ancient arcane runes, forged from a Nether Star, Dragon's Breath, and Lapis Lazuli.
  - **Offhand Mechanism**:
    - When placed in the offhand, opening an Enchanting Table adds a dedicated "Reroll Enchantments" button (with a green-gold circular arrow icon) directly beneath the lapis lazuli slot.
  - **Experience-Based Enchantment Reroll**:
    - With an enchantable item placed in the enchanting slot, clicking the button consumes 1 experience level (free in Creative mode, no lapis lazuli required) to regenerate the player's enchantment seed and instantly reroll all three enchantment options and clues.
  - **Intelligent Protection & Tooltip Guidance**:
    - Disabled with tooltip "Place an enchantable item first" if the slot is empty or the item is unenchantable.
    - Disabled with tooltip "Not enough experience (Requires Level 1)" if the player lacks the required experience level.
    - Active with tooltip "Reroll Enchantments (Cost: 1 Level)" when conditions are met, preventing accidental loss of levels.
  - **Crafting Recipe**:
    - Shaped crafting: Row 1 `[ Empty, Lapis Block, Empty ]`, Row 2 `[ Nether Star, Book, Dragon's Breath ]`, Row 3 `[ Empty, Lapis Block, Empty ]`.
  - **Ecosystem Integration**:
    - Added to the `tuanzis_mod_tab` creative inventory tab and registered with comprehensive JEI documentation.

## New Weapons
- **New Weapon: Pulse Resonance Sword**:
  - A mechanical relic forged in the deepest ruins of the Redstone Institute by a forgotten mechanist using brass gears and pulse coils. The blade was not designed to cut, but to conduct.
  - **Base Attributes**:
    - Base Attack Damage: 6.
    - Attack Speed: 2.5 (Rapid-attack focus).
    - Base DPS: 6 × 2.5 = 15.0.
  - **Pulse Charging Mechanics**:
    - Each successful melee attack automatically adds 1 stack of "Pulse" to the internal capacitor (**cannot build capacitor pulse when attack charge is < 80%** to enforce proper rhythmic pacing).
    - Upon reaching 3 stacks, the next attack unleashes an additional **Pulse Shock**, dealing 50% base attack damage (3 extra damage) to the primary target (calculated via DamageCalculator), resetting pulse stacks to 0.
    - Unleashing the pulse shock triggers high-voltage electric overload sound effects and lightning spark particles. Charging produces escalating pitch chimes with subtle electric arcs.
  - **Wear System & Gradient Rendering**:
    - Wear value (0.0001–1.0) is randomly determined upon creation and permanently fixed.
    - Features 5 appearance tiers: Coil Calibrated, Capacitor Leaking, Gear Lagging, Insulation Damaged, Core Meltdown.
    - Implemented via Range Dispatch and custom dual-layer dynamic Alpha TintSource, providing smooth material transitions and overheated reddish tones at stage 5.
    - Attack and overload sound pitches shift lower with increased wear.
  - **Creative Inventory & Tag Integration**: Registered in `tuanzis_mod_tab` and `minecraft:swords` tag.
  - **Default/Unidentified Texture Optimization**: Aligned the default/fallback texture with "Bee Sting Echo" to display the iconic unidentified black sword silhouette with a yellow question mark.
  - **JEI Support**: Registered with full JEI info description pages.

## New Enchantments
- **New Enchantment Category: Ancient Scroll (古卷附魔)**:
  - **Exclusive Aesthetics**: All Ancient Scroll enchantments render their name and level suffix in a gentle lavender purple (`#0x7F00FF`), formatted with the naming convention `Ancient Scroll - [Name]` (`古卷附魔-xxxx`).
  - **Strict Acquisition**: Ancient Scroll enchantments can only appear naturally in the Enchanting Table. They cannot be obtained through villager trading, loot chests, or mob spawn equipment.
  - **Level 30 Enchanting Table Requirement**: Strictly requires an Enchanting Table level of 30 or higher (requiring 15 bookshelves at the third slot); additionally, the Enchanting Table can only ever roll Level I.
  - **Anvil Upgrading**: Higher enchantment levels must be achieved by combining two Level I items or enchanted books on an Anvil.
- **New Ancient Scroll Enchantment: Ancient Scroll - Blood Leech (古卷附魔-汲血)**:
  - **Applicable Gear**: Swords and Axes (Sharp melee weapons).
  - **Max Level**: Level IV.
  - **Rarity**: Highest rarity (weight = 1).
  - **Core Effect**: Dealing melee damage restores the attacker's health based on `5% + 5% × Level` of the final damage dealt (Level I: 10%, Level II: 15%, Level III: 20%, Level IV: 25%).
  - **Feedback & Ecosystem**: Spawns heart particles and outputs `ModLog` debug logs on successful leech; added Level I and Level IV enchanted books to the creative tab; registered with full JEI info pages.
- **New Normalization Combat Enchantment: Overload Protocol**:
  - **Core Mechanics**: Attacks have a `(5% + 5% × Level)` chance to stack 1 "Pulse" on the weapon (identical to the Pulse Resonance Sword pulse mechanics, max 3 stacks; also subject to the attack charge >= 80% threshold; when applied to the Pulse Resonance Sword, removes chance-based pulse gain and keeps guaranteed 1-stack gain). Upon reaching 3 stacks, the next melee attack deals extra Electric Shock Damage: base fixed damage adjusted to `Base Weapon Damage × (10% + 5% × Level)` plus `+0.85` damage per level (stacks with intrinsic +3.0 electric passive damage on the Pulse Resonance Sword), resetting pulse stacks to 0.
  - **Universal Weapon/Tool Support**: Effective on all melee weapons and tools, not bound to any specific weapon, and not restricted by combat environment.
  - **Normalization Exclusion**: Belongs to the Normalization Enchantment system (`EXCLUSIVE_NORMALIZATION`), mutually exclusive with Resonance Pulse, Buzzing Rhythm, Abyssal Rhythm, Void Resonance, and Steel Shield Gift.
  - **Ecosystem Integration**: Added Level V enchanted book to the creative inventory tab, registered with full JEI description pages, and supports holding Shift to view current pulse charges on any weapon.

## Optimizations & Adjustments
- **World Sculptor's Pen & Color Block Series Compatibility Optimization**:
  - **Full Color Data Inheritance**: Holding the World Sculptor's Pen and pressing Shift + Right Click on Color Blocks, Color Slabs (supporting independent top/bottom color detection on double slabs), or Color Stairs automatically extracts and stores their exact color data (RGB).
  - **Dynamic Preview & Tooltip**: The miniature block icon rendered at the lower-right corner of the pen texture and hover tooltips dynamically display the dyed color and hex value.
  - **Smart Inventory Check & Deduction**: Batch filling precisely inspects and consumes matching colored blocks from the player's inventory and shulker boxes, preventing unintended consumption of uncolored/white or differently colored blocks.
  - **Accurate Dyed Placement**: Automatically updates placed block entities with the selected color instead of placing default white variants.
  - **Color-Preserving Undo & Anti-Tamper**: Undoing a fill action verifies that block colors have not been tampered with and returns items to the player with their exact dyed color components intact;
  - **Block NBT & Tile Entity Data Restoration**: Fixed a bug where undoing a fill only restored the BlockState, losing tile entity NBT data. The pen now captures full tile entity metadata before filling (clearing Clearable containers to prevent duplication exploits), and fully restores them on undo using `loadWithComponents` with immediate client-side sync;
  - **Interaction Logic Optimization**: Optimized the Shift + Right-click interaction on the World Sculptor's Pen. Sneak (Shift) + Right-clicking a block now 100% prioritizes selecting or changing the fill block, preventing accidental undo triggers during the 60-second undo window. The undo action is now triggered exclusively by Sneak (Shift) + Right-clicking in the air.
- **Enchantment & Loot Balance**:
  - **Normalization Enchantment Acquisition**: All normalization combat enchantments (Resonance Pulse, Buzzing Rhythm, Abyssal Rhythm, Void Resonance, Steel Shield Gift, Overload Protocol) have been removed from vanilla enchanting tables, villager trades, and random loot chests. They are strictly reserved as exclusive system enchantments.
- **Equipment Anvil Repair Materials**:
  - **Bee Sting Echo**: Repair material changed from Warden Heart to vanilla **Honeycomb Block**;
  - **Rift Scar**: Repair material changed from Eye of Ender to vanilla **Echo Shard**;
  - **Pulse Resonance Sword**: Repair material changed to vanilla **Amethyst Block**;
  - Updated corresponding JEI maintenance and repair description notes.
- **Weapon & Combat Attributes**:
  - **Shuriken Damage Boost**: Shuriken base projectile damage increased from 2 to 4, with JEI description updated accordingly.
- **Boss Mechanics & Status Effects**:
  - **Wither & Ender Dragon Status Effect Immunity Removed**: Removed hardcoded immunity on the Wither and Ender Dragon against mod effects ("Tearing", "Bee Poison", "Bee Poison Cooldown", "Resonance", "Tide Erosion"). They can now be afflicted by these effects and take periodic bleed/erosion damage.
- **Entity Drop Mechanics**:
  - **Charged Creeper Drop**: When a player is killed by a charged creeper, it now drops a "Chromatic Skull" instead of the player skin head.
- **Trial Dummy Placement Optimization**:
  - **Placement Distance and Count Restriction Removed**: Removed the restriction where each player was limited to placing at most 1 trial dummy within a 10-block radius. Players can now freely place multiple trial dummies in the same area without automatically recovering nearby dummies, facilitating side-by-side DPS comparisons and training setups.
