# Tuanzi's Mod 1.0.7.2 Changelog

## Mechanics & QoL Optimizations

- **Weapon Mechanics Optimization: Nodachi**:
  - **Extended Attack Interval Timeout**:
    - The attack interval timeout before triggering Momentum exhaustion has been significantly increased from **1.5 seconds (30 ticks)** to **5.0 seconds (100 ticks)**.
    - Drastically improves fluidity and fault tolerance during combat, allowing players ample time to reposition, align sweet-spot blade hits, and time rhythm strikes without easily falling into exhaustion weakness.
  - **Blade Sweet Spot Damage Adjustments**:
    - **Blade Tip (>= 2.2 blocks)**: Now deals **1.15x (115%)** damage (previously 100%) and accumulates Momentum, rewarding precise distance management.
    - **Middle Blade (1.5 ~ 2.2 blocks)**: Now deals **100%** full base damage (previously 85%) and maintains Momentum.
    - **Close Hilt (0 ~ 1.5 blocks)**: Now deals **85%** damage (15% penalty, previously 25% penalty), cannot build Momentum, and knockback is significantly reduced.
  - **Tooltip & Documentation Parity**:
    - Synchronized all Nodachi in-game hover tooltips and JEI detailed mechanics documentation to reflect the new 5-second penalty timeout and sweet spot damage ratios.
